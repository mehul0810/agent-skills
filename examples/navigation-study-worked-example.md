# A navigation miss is a clue, not a diagnosis

A theatre suspects people cannot retrieve their booking on a phone. The team has two explanations: visitors don't understand its menu, or they don't notice where to open it. A new link color might help one and do nothing for the other. This is a fictional study design, not a report of visitor behavior.

**Task to show a participant:** “You paid for two seats for Saturday. You're at the entrance and can't find the confirmation email. What would you try?”

**Key for the observer only:** The current path is **Orders > Retrieve confirmation**. Include plausible alternatives such as **What's on**, **Plan your visit** and **Contact**. Don't show the key or ask someone to “find Retrieve confirmation.”

| Question | Smallest useful comparison | What to do with the result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do the words and grouping make sense? | Try the neutral task in the plain text tree. Record the first choice, wrong turns and requests for help. | If people take the same wrong branch, compare one alternative label or grouping against the same neighbors. Don't restructure the whole site on one observation. |
| Is the route visible in context? | Try the same task and labels in the rendered mobile menu. Use fresh comparable participants, or equivalent tasks in a counterbalanced order; a second attempt can teach the answer. | If the text tree works but the page doesn't, inspect what the closed menu hides. Compare one change to placement, disclosure or contrast, keeping the others stable. The difference alone doesn't identify its cause. |
| Can someone actually recover their booking? | Follow the chosen link through the retrieval flow with a permitted test booking, including an invalid entry and a successful confirmation. | Separate the first selection, recovery and confirmed outcome. An engineer finishing a fixture does not establish that visitors can do so unaided. |

No new tracking, recruitment, live customer email or real booking is needed to write this plan. Obtain the applicable authorization before running a study. For a bounded copy correction with an unchanged destination, just make and verify that edit.
