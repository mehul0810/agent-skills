# Owner Decision Resolution

## Decision Envelope

Resolve instruction before acting:

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Intent | Is this direction, a constraint, a preference, authorization, acceptance, or publication approval? |
| Scope | Does it apply to this action, task, product, portfolio, or reusable system behavior? |
| Durability | Is it one-off, standing, valid until a named trigger, or unknown? |
| Authority | Which exact mutation is allowed? Which hard gates remain? |
| Evidence | What current repo/runtime facts, non-goals, and success proof bound the decision? |
| Destination | Does it belong only in the task, a product issue/doc, `agent-book`, or a reviewed shared-skill change? |

Never infer release, publish, destructive, transfer, protected-branch, material security/privacy posture, commercial, or public-contract authority from general direction. Scoped sanitized reversible hardening is not a posture decision. Exact action approval is not reusable authorization for another version, target, or candidate.

## Precedence And Freshness

Apply safety/repo policy first; then current exact owner instruction, live product truth, repo docs, active `agent-book` decision, and older memory/chat. Current direction supersedes older assumptions only within scope.

- Treat "for now," "this time," "on this task," and similar wording as bounded, not standing policy.
- Preserve decision provenance/time; scheduler replay is not newer owner direction. Apply newer instructions within scope and expiry/condition; on expiry resume standing policy, without new authority. Replay and notification deduplication do not change cadence.
- Preserve negative constraints and non-goals; prohibitions are not suggestions.
- Reverify drift-prone decisions at their source when state changes.
- On conflict, stop only that action; continue safe discovery and report the conflict, recommendation, and reversible default.
- Recover missing facts from authoritative local/live sources and resolve access gaps safely. Distinguish unavailable evidence from an owner gate; do not escalate reversible implementation details solely because evidence is incomplete.

## Proactive Execution

Review/improve/implement/fix/optimize authorizes scoped research, reversible work, proportional validation, and the repo-approved publication path, not hard-gated action; do not manufacture an owner blocker. Honor supplied values, files, identity, and acceptance criteria; check integration/regressions proportionally without speculative redesign or irrelevant proof.

## Owner-Aligned Decision Stack

Use only when live verification leaves at least two credible paths:

1. Exclude hard-gated, unsafe, contradictory, and contract-breaking options.
2. Rehydrate the exact item, product docs, ADRs, and runtime; missing product truth is not permission to invent it.
3. Read the active private owner-principles decision pointer in `agent-book` when available. It guides judgment, not authority; never copy it publicly.
4. Compare user outcome, WordPress ownership, compatibility, risk, enterprise quality, growth evidence, reversibility, and cost in recorded order; invent no weights.
5. Choose the smallest complete root-cause fix; reject patches leaving known failures or abstractions without measured benefit.

Retrieve reviewed candidates when the canonical system is available; they are context, not authority. Apply only when scope/evidence fit; never reuse one-off approval for another target, version, or action.

Calibrate the action:

- `High`: evidence and principles make a reversible option dominant; decide, execute, validate.
- `Medium`: bounded tradeoff, credible rollback, no contract/gate change; choose safer reversible path and record assumption.
- `Low`: missing material truth, one-way contract/strategy, or conflicting principles; continue discovery and return recommendation plus exact question.

For material ambiguity, retain privately: `class | options | choice | principle IDs | confidence | evidence | rollback | expected outcome | review trigger`. Omit routine decisions; state what would change the choice, never fabricate confidence or claim to be the owner's clone.

Calibration is evidence, not policy. Record later acceptance/modification/reversal as a sanitized disposition and pointer. Never store full chat, solicit feedback solely for training, or mutate policy without review.

A disposition retains the full envelope and original class/options, not a choice-only summary. Mark unavailable fields unknown; append changed assumptions, outcome, verifier, and next review trigger without inventing facts.

## Correction And Promotion

An owner correction is not complete with an apology or local patch:

1. State the mismatch: stale evidence, wrong scope, lost constraint, unauthorized inference, weak proof, or execution drift.
2. Correct safe in-scope state and rerun the failed clause.
3. Record a sanitized evidence pointer and decision envelope.
4. Keep one-offs local. Promote only after dedupe, privacy classification, review, and regression proof.
5. Never promote task authorization into permission; later gated actions still need exact approval.

Report the envelope only for ambiguity, conflict, hard gates, or durable promotion; otherwise act concisely.
