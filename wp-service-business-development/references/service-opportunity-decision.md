# Service Opportunity Decision

Use for a WordPress consulting or agency opportunity after the service-business lane is selected. This is a commercial decision aid, not a sales script or permission to contact, commit, or sign for the owner.

## Evidence Ledger

Record each material input as `confirmed`, `buyer-stated`, `inferred`, or `unknown`:

- account/buyer role and the user group affected;
- current problem, workaround, consequence, trigger date, and desired outcome;
- decision process, budget reality, technical/access constraints, stakeholders, procurement, and competitor/incumbent context when actually known;
- relevant proof: analytics, support history, discovery notes, repository/runtime evidence, prior work, or supplied artifacts;
- delivery capacity, dependency owners, risks, and the next decision needed.

Do not fill empty fields with plausible market or client stories. One friendly call, a title, or an inbound request does not prove fit, budget, authority, or urgency.

## Qualification Decision

Classify the current opportunity by evidence, not enthusiasm:

| Status | Use when | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `advance` | Problem, buyer fit, outcome, access, and feasible commercial path are sufficiently clear. | Make one bounded proposal or decision request. |
| `discovery` | The problem seems material but scope, root cause, decision path, or evidence is missing. | Offer a paid or explicitly bounded discovery with a decision deliverable. |
| `nurture` | Fit is plausible but timing, authority, or urgency is not active. | Preserve context and define a non-intrusive future trigger. |
| `decline` | Fit, ethics, capacity, risk, or commercial path is poor. | Give a concise reason and, if appropriate, a safer alternative. |
| `blocked` | A required fact, owner approval, access, or safety constraint prevents a responsible path. | Name the exact gap and the smallest safe resolution. |

Use a lightweight comparison, not a fake precision score. Consider outcome size, confidence, urgency, strategic fit, reversibility, delivery/maintenance cost, capacity, dependency risk, and ability to prove progress. State the factors that changed the decision.

## Offer And Scope Boundary

Lead with the buyer outcome and observable success, then state the mechanism. A complete commercial shape includes:

1. audience/problem and expected outcome;
2. in-scope deliverables and explicit non-goals;
3. discovery assumptions, client responsibilities, access, dependencies, and third-party costs;
4. acceptance evidence or a learning decision, including what cannot be promised;
5. delivery phases and decision gates; avoid calendar certainty before dependencies are proven;
6. commercial shape: fixed scope, time-and-materials, retainer, or paid discovery—with the rationale;
7. change-control trigger, risk/rollback path, and the next approval.

Price a known scope; price discovery when the problem or boundary is not known. Use a range when material assumptions remain. Every range states the assumptions that would move it and the next fact needed to narrow it. Discounting, special terms, revenue share, guarantees, and capacity commitments require the owner’s explicit decision.

## Proposal And Retainer Outputs

For a proposal, write an owner-reviewable decision document first. Only produce client-facing wording when asked. Preserve uncertainty and do not write legal obligations as though they were agreed.

For an ongoing retainer, define the recurring outcome (for example, release readiness, performance risk reduction, or conversion experiment support), monthly/quarterly cadence, included and excluded work, queue/priority rule, client inputs, quality/proof gates, escalation, review metrics, and renewal decision. “Anything WordPress” is not a defensible scope.

For capacity, compare candidate work by expected value and confidence alongside effort, delivery risk, maintenance load, and strategic fit. Prefer small validation or discovery when it resolves the assumption most likely to change the decision. Do not let a short-term paid request crowd out a current committed obligation without an explicit tradeoff.

## Handoff And Record

Store the evidence ledger and owner-approved decision in the authorized CRM, project brief, or repository artifact. Route marketing artifact work to `content-writer`, delivery to the owning WordPress specialist, and product strategy to the PO. Re-open the decision if scope, evidence, budget, access, stakeholder, capacity, or risk materially changes.
