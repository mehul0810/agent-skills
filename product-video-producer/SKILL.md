# Product Video Producer

Turn product truth, brand assets, and visual references into restrained enterprise video with an auditable storyboard, deterministic outputs, and a bounded review loop.

## Route

- Reference review, brand translation, storyboard, typography, pacing, composition, motion, or abstract storytelling: read `references/reference-to-storyboard.md`.
- Rendering, 1080p/4K delivery, poster, manifest, QA, or user-critique iteration: read `references/render-review-iteration.md`.
- Use Creative Production or `imagegen` only for supporting visual exploration/assets. Preserve exact logos, product UI, typography, and copy from governed source files; composite them deterministically rather than asking generation to reproduce them.
- Product claims/copy may route to `content-writer`; WordPress surfaces route to the relevant WP specialist. This skill owns the video artifact and proof.

## Workflow

1. Confirm objective, audience, channel, duration, deliverable profile, approved claims, brand kit, reference files/URLs, product evidence, audio/licensing constraints, and review owner.
2. Inspect the actual references. Record timestamped observations and distinguish observed cues, inferred intent, reusable principle, and elements that must not be copied.
3. Preflight logos, fonts, colors, screenshots/captures, generated imagery, music, voice, and licenses. Reject missing transparency, accidental matte backgrounds, low-resolution sources, or unverified product states.
4. Produce a shot-level storyboard with copy, evidence, assets, motion, transition, duration, and acceptance criteria. When direction is not already approved, obtain storyboard or animatic approval before the full-quality render.
5. Build from an explicit timeline and render contract. Keep product truth separate from abstract metaphor; never fabricate product UI, customers, metrics, capabilities, or outcomes.
6. Deliver the review render, intentional poster frame, and manifest. Validate technical metadata, content, typography, logo edges, motion, audio, accessibility, and claims.
7. Convert critique into timestamped changes and invariants. Revise affected shots first, preserve approved decisions, rerender deterministically, and report remaining proof gaps.

## Non-Negotiables

- Reference work is analysis, not imitation. Do not reproduce a competitor's sequence, distinctive visual identity, music, copy, or protected assets.
- No white-box logo artifacts: use the approved SVG or alpha asset, validate edges over light and dark frames, and do not redraw a brand mark with generative tools.
- Abstract-product storytelling may explain a verified problem, workflow, control, or outcome, but must not imply unshipped interfaces or unsupported claims.
- Prefer typography, hierarchy, space, timing, and purposeful transitions over ornamental motion. Premium does not mean constant movement.
- Do not publish, release, upload, or overwrite an approved master without explicit owner approval.

## Required Handoff

Report the approved direction, storyboard/version, render profile, output paths, source/asset provenance, claims evidence, validation results, critique changes, unresolved gaps, and approval still required.
