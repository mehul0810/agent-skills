# Channel Delivery And Feedback

Use this for software promos, YouTube long-form, Shorts/cutdowns, thumbnails/titles, publication packages, or post-publication learning.

## Channel Contract

Define the audience, one honest content promise, viewing context, primary action, duration class, aspect ratio, and measurement plan before editing. One master timeline may supply derivatives, but each derivative needs a native composition and editorial pass. Do not center-crop a desktop demo into a Short and call it finished.

### Enterprise Promo Or Explainer

- Lead with the operational problem or outcome, then show credible product/workflow proof, control, failure/recovery behavior, and a specific next action.
- Prefer a real product sequence, customer-approved evidence, or clearly labeled concept over generic SaaS montage.
- Prepare the requested web/event/sales aspect ratios from governed compositions. Validate legibility at the actual embed/player size and supply a static poster/reduced-motion fallback for autoplay surfaces.

### YouTube Long-Form

- Make the opening fulfill the title/thumbnail promise immediately. Remove throat-clearing; establish audience, outcome, and why the viewer should continue.
- Structure around viewer questions and proof: context, demonstration/explanation, constraints or failure handling, result, next action. Use chapters when they improve navigation.
- For educational or tutorial claims, keep a dated source ledger tied to script/timecodes, prefer current primary sources, distinguish fact from inference, and prepare a clear correction when material guidance changes; applying a public correction remains owner-gated.
- Reserve a clean end-screen composition and enough hold time. Prepare description links, attribution, captions, disclosure decision, and a truthful thumbnail/title set; uploading remains owner-gated.

### YouTube Shorts

- Reframe natively for vertical/square viewing: larger UI crops, fewer simultaneous elements, deliberate caption zones, and mobile-safe focal placement. Rewrite the opening and close for the Short rather than compressing the long-form script.
- Live-check current duration/classification, music eligibility, thumbnail-frame behavior, upload resolution, and analytics definitions before delivery. Platform rules are not permanent skill constants.

## Delivery Profiles

For YouTube SDR delivery, live-verify the official encoding table and use the high-quality source profile it specifies: MP4, H.264 High Profile, progressive scan, native frame rate, VBR, 4:2:0, BT.709, and AAC-LC or Opus at 48 kHz. Do not add baked black bars or upscale weak sources to claim 4K. Keep a mezzanine/master separate from platform derivatives when the project justifies it.

An intentional poster is an internal/embed still from the approved video. A YouTube thumbnail is external promise packaging: truthful, high-resolution, mobile-legible, brand-specific, and visually complementary to the title rather than a duplicate sentence. Keep Shorts selected-frame behavior separate from long-form custom-thumbnail handling.

## Rights, Accessibility, And Disclosure

- Deliver human-reviewed timed captions with speaker changes and meaningful non-speech audio. Provide a transcript and visual-equivalence/audio-description decision when essential UI or visual information is not conveyed by narration.
- Record ownership/license and attribution for music, footage, fonts, stock, customer marks, voice, and likeness. Check current Content ID implications before delivery.
- Record whether realistic media, people, places, events, voices, or music were meaningfully generated or altered. Apply the destination's current disclosure control when required; do not rely only on embedded C2PA metadata or assume minor production assistance needs the same treatment.
- Never expose secrets, customer data, internal URLs, unreleased UI, or test credentials in a public frame, caption, transcript, poster, or thumbnail.

## Pre-Publication Package

Before approval, return without uploading:

1. Exact review/master/derivative identities and hashes.
2. Platform profile and current-spec verification date/source.
3. Caption/transcript/audio-description artifacts and QA.
4. Thumbnail/title options, poster, description/chapters/CTA, and end-screen plan.
5. Rights/attribution and synthetic-media disclosure decisions.
6. Full watch-through, desktop/mobile playback, and platform-transcode proof plan.
7. Known proof gaps and exact owner approval still required.

## Authorized Publication

After explicit approval for the exact target and artifact:

1. Reverify the job, project root, expected artifact ID/role/path/hash, and destination account/channel.
2. Upload only that artifact and apply the approved title, thumbnail, description, chapters, captions, rights attribution, and disclosure state.
3. Record the published URL/ID, applied metadata version, time, and operator.
4. Validate the processed platform transcode, captions, thumbnail, color/audio, links, and access state. If publication partially fails, stop further mutations, preserve the local package, and report the exact recovery or rollback action.

Starting, stopping, or applying a live title/thumbnail experiment changes public packaging and requires explicit owner approval. Preparation and analysis may proceed without it.

## Evidence-Led Learning

After publication, use sufficient evidence rather than taste:

- Check whether title/thumbnail expectation matches the opening, key retention moments, dips/spikes, traffic source, and conversion evidence.
- For Shorts, prefer engaged-view and stayed-to-watch signals over raw view count alone when those metrics are available.
- Propose YouTube's native title/thumbnail tests for eligible long-form work when useful; it evaluates watch time, not click-through rate alone. Do not activate or apply a test without explicit owner approval.
- Record the observation, sample window, hypothesis, one variable to test, expected signal, result, and next decision. Do not redesign from low-sample noise or silently mutate the reusable skill from one video's performance.
