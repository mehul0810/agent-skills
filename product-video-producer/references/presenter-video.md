# Creator And Avatar Video

Start with **who is speaking and what they can truthfully know**. A paid creator can demonstrate a feature; an actual customer can recount an evidenced experience in their approved words. A fictional brand host or licensed stock avatar can explain a workflow, not claim to have bought the product, used it for a month or achieved a result. Do not manufacture a review by adding a synthetic label. Creator-style framing is an editorial choice, not evidence of an independent recommendation.

## Choose The Talent

| What is available? | Shoot or prepare | Do not assume |
|---|---|---|
| Consenting human creator | Brief the claim and required proof; leave room for their own accurate phrasing, actions and retakes. | Payment does not buy a digital double, voice clone or perpetual reuse. |
| Recognizable person or digital twin | Check documented permission for **face and voice separately**, this script/use, channels, paid/organic, languages, term and provider. Follow the provider's own participant-consent process. | A public reel, recording, employment relationship or release for one locale is not permission for cloning, dubbing or vendor transfer. |
| Fictional or licensed stock presenter | Check character, voice and commercial license. Make the host's role clear and use brand-owned factual copy. | Stock access does not authorize an identifiable lookalike or a customer testimonial. |

If rights are missing, continue with a paper edit, cleared product footage, type-led cut, or a clearly different licensed host; hold only the affected performance. Do not send a face, voice, or consent recording to a new service, enroll a twin, buy credits, or publish without the relevant authorization. Keep permission references private; the public handoff needs the clearance decision, not raw identity evidence. [HeyGen's consent process](https://help.heygen.com/en/articles/12092609-recording-your-consent-video) is a provider example, not a required tool.

## Direct The Performance

Write a two-column paper edit: **host line / what the viewer sees that makes it true**. For example: "Here are the two versions" over the host; cut to the cleared side-by-side comparison on "two"; return to the host for the choice. Avoid a talking head reciting a feature list or cutting to B-roll exactly when the evidence appears.

For a human shoot, give one intention per take, a lens eyeline, repeatable framing and clean pauses for edits. For an avatar, use short thought units; select a representative line with a pause, product term and gesture. Check its visual performance: mouth closures, gaze, hands, edge shimmer and identity across cuts. Cut to real product evidence rather than hiding an unsupported claim behind the face. A visual sample is not verified narration. Keep captions, disclosure, gestures and the demonstrated control from covering one another.

## Two Independent Disclosure Decisions

- **Commercial relationship:** identify sponsor, gift, employment or affiliate relationship where relevant. Plan plain, conspicuous wording with the endorsement in each applicable cut/language; a description-only note or a platform toggle is not a universal substitute. The [US FTC's influencer guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers) and [avatar/testimonial FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers) distinguish disclosure from a truthful underlying claim.
- **Synthetic identity or media:** separately record whether the presenter, voice or event is realistically synthesized or altered and which audience/placement rules apply. For Indian influencer advertising, [ASCI's guidance](https://www.ascionline.in/the-asci-code-guidelines/) calls for a prominent upfront explanation that a virtual influencer is not human. Check [YouTube AI disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491), or TikTok's separate [AI](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content) and [commercial](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service) policies for that destination. Labels do not cure deception or permission gaps.

Reviewed 2026-11-22. Recheck the actual market, destination and provider before delivery. For a paid fictional presenter, the two decisions may both apply; an unpaid, real presenter in a private training cut need not inherit an ad treatment.
