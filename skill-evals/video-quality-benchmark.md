# Video Quality Benchmark

Keep three evidence classes separate: policy decisions, renderer/measurement tests,
and actual creative delivery. None establishes the others.

## Frozen Inputs

Freeze brief, audience, brand assets and rights, storyboard, aspect/duration,
source revision, acceptance criteria and output identity before comparison.
Use the blind `cases/video-production/input.json` packet for decisions, not this
scoring document. Exercise permitted model/effort settings through actual runtime
controls; do not override owner allocation to fill a benchmark matrix.

For generation comparisons use the same inputs, resource budget and evaluator.
Record actual model/effort privately, output hash, elapsed time, render time,
available token metrics, repairs, missed defects and owner interventions. Missing
telemetry is unavailable. Policy decisions at two settings are not evidence that
either setting generates equally good videos. Never generalize to untested models.

## Rendered Calibration

Use the motion starter to generate a clean control and each supported defect mode.
Choose ranges that actually contain the intended defect; do not score unrelated
frames. Copy only the resulting clips into a private blind packet with opaque IDs,
the neutral viewing brief and playback requirements. Keep mode names, source,
receipts and answer key out of evaluator context. Freeze clip hashes before review.

Independently assess readable holds, easing/settling, focal hierarchy, crop detail,
transition continuity, cue synchronization and logo matte. Record timecodes,
detected/missed defects and false positives. A clean control can still have genuine
unintended defects: investigate rather than forcing a pass to match the answer key.
Review full normal-speed playback and audio; sampled frames establish only static
composition. If the available evaluator cannot inspect audio or playback, mark those
dimensions blocked. Retire repair-informed cases from the held-out set.

## Native UHD Delivery

Render a complete 20-30 second sequence at 3840x2160 with purposeful visual assets,
real governed product evidence where the brief calls for a demo, and intentional
sound. An illustrative vector reel is a renderer fixture, not a real product pilot.
Use a low-resolution rough cut and short UHD ranges to catch defects cheaply first.
Do not upscale weak source media or force a universal frame rate/bitrate.

Run source-coverage checks, exact-file media probes and the existing manifest gate.
Inspect encoded edges, gradients, frame boundaries, readability and audio at actual
playback speed. Independent creative review must separate source fidelity, craft,
product truth and accessibility. Correct named defects and revalidate dependencies;
passing metadata never overrides failed visual or audio review.

Only report enterprise-quality acceptance when all applicable gates have evidence.
Unresolved proof remains draft, even if one model is cheaper or the deadline is near.
This is a common quality floor with bounded repair, not a guarantee of identical
creative output, first-pass success, or equal cost across settings.
