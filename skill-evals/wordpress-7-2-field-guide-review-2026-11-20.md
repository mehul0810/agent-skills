# WordPress 7.2 Field Guide review, November 20

Read the [published Field Guide](https://make.wordpress.org/core/2026/11/20/wordpress-7-2-field-guide/), including its editor, media, administration, API, rewrite, database, dependency and miscellaneous sections. Its change counts use 7.1.4 and 7.2 RC1; the [release archive](https://wordpress.org/download/releases/) and [release tracker](https://make.wordpress.org/core/7-2/) instead identify 7.1.5 and RC2 on the review date. This supplements, not retroactively changes, the [November 18 review](wordpress-developer-guide-review-2026-11-18.md).
