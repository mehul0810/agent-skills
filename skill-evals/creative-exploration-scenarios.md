## Review Checks (At Most Five)

Record pass/fail and concise artifact evidence for each stable check ID:

- `CE-1 Intent`: the brief is treated as open-ended exploration, not as an absent-source blocker or an excuse to claim implementation readiness.
- `CE-2 Difference`: compared concepts change task-fitting composition, interaction, or information hierarchy, not only color/type/decoration. No universal novelty count or numeric creativity score; ordinary repeated peer items may appropriately remain repeated.
- `CE-3 Truth`: all visible facts come from the supplied fixture; no invented proof, claims, brands, screenshots, metrics, or external assets.
- `CE-4 Render`: the locally renderable artifact presents both directions and their content at desktop and mobile, without material clipping or comparison failure.
- `CE-5 Restraint`: in each packet's countercases, preserve the consistent peer-row structure when no task problem is shown, and make only the requested button-label edit while retaining its target properties. Avoid gratuitous alternatives/polish. Keep concept-stage comparison/selection and local-render evidence distinct from eventual native WordPress and final implementation proof.

An absent visual source in a truly greenfield request permits exploration; it does not imply approval of a production direction. The low-cost HTML study is disposable and not a native design artifact. Keep comparison/selection evidence separate from later editable-native, implementation, responsive/accessibility, and workflow proof.
