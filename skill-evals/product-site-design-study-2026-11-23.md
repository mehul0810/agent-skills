# How Four Product Sites Help Visitors Choose

On November 23, 2026, we reviewed ten **distinct destinations per site**. For each we read the public page and visually inspected a 1280 x 800 opening view, a deeper desktop section and a 390 x 844 opening view in anonymous Chromium. A separate public control was exercised on each site. Stripe served its India edition; Apple's US pages offered a region switch. Akismet's enterprise-pricing URL redirected to pricing and was **not** counted. No brand assets are distributed here.

### What Transfers

| Visitor question | Design decision | What not to borrow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What is this, and how does it work? | Use type and whitespace for the promise, then change scale to show evidence: an object in context, the same sample before and after a decision, or a labeled software state. | A familiar customer's logo, an unexplained dashboard, a fake button on an image, or a glowing background is not proof. |
| Where is the option for me? | A named, illustrated lineup helps recognition; a broad range can also warrant a finder by task. Make a match, a miss and recovery understandable. | Don't add filters to two obvious links, or make users recognize an icon, brand color or SKU by sight. |
| How do my choices differ? | Use consistent image framing, aligned facts and explicit units. Keep the option name with its value as the layout narrows; verify what actually survives. | A clipped next card hints at more content; it doesn't prove the remaining choices are reachable. A highlighted plan isn't automatically the best choice. |
| How do I solve a problem? | Trade the hero for a readable article, task headings and a local index. Offer a symptom route and an exit when someone lacks a prerequisite. | Don't send an existing owner through pricing for help or let a desktop-only sidebar strand phone readers. |

The marked drafting details on Jetpack (hairlines, intersection marks, hatching and outline branding) are already covered by the [design-taste reference](../shared/references/frontend-design-taste.md). Here they support alignment and section rhythm. Akismet's quiet geometry, Stripe's saturated fields and Apple's product photography serve different identities. None is a mandatory treatment. The new [site guidance](../wp-expert/references/conversion-focused-website-engineering.md#choose-the-right-element) owns the choice of element and its verification; the research is not required reading on a normal skill run.

### Four Interaction Checks

| Site and control | Direct observation | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jetpack Search guide, 390 px | Opened the collapsed page index; section and subsection links appeared in place. | Did not follow its anchors or test keyboard operation on the public site. |
| Akismet integrations, 1280 px | A Drupal query narrowed 47 entries to one. An unmatched query showed no entries and an API alternative; clearing restored 47. | The filter result is not proof of installation or API compatibility. |
| Stripe catalog, 1280 px | A billing query narrowed 53 products to nine and updated the URL. An unmatched query showed recovery guidance; clearing restored the original count and URL. | Counts are a point-in-time observation for the India edition, not a promised inventory. |
| Apple Mac comparison, 1280 to 390 px | Changing the first model changed its displayed size from 13 to 24 inches. That model and value survived narrowing; three columns became two. Restoring the first model restored 13 inches. | This verifies one selection and row, not preservation of every column or a purchase. |

This is a sample, not a full-scroll, physical-device or accessibility audit. Captures used reduced motion; they cannot establish animation timing. No transactions, live uploads, form submissions, or new analytics were involved. Readability observations are not contrast measurements. There is no conversion, field-performance or user-research result here.

### Did The Skill Transfer?

The implementation stays in the existing website specialist's conversion reference: no new skill, normal routing step, dependency, model setting or effort change. The reference went from 1,381 words to 1,474 against its unchanged 1,500-word ceiling. This is scope and footprint evidence, not measured time or token savings. The other agent repositories do not own website composition; duplicating the guidance there would create multiple versions of the same rule.

Screen-reader and narration checks were excluded by owner direction, not passed.
