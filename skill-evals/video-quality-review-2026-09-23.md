# Video Quality Implementation Review

## Scope

This update establishes a common acceptance contract across permitted model and
reasoning settings. It does not promise identical creative output or first-pass
success. Existing product truth, rights, privacy, accessibility, output identity
and publication gates remain intact. Delegated creative selection can proceed
through internal review; explicit owner checkpoints still apply.

The optional vector starter is illustrative renderer/calibration infrastructure,
not a real product demo or a mandatory visual style. Its synthetic cue is not a
finished sound mix. Generated files are drafts until applicable production gates
have actual evidence. No cloud renderer, new paid resource or global dependency
was installed.

## Review Findings Repaired

- Sub-1 zoom values could make a tiny crop falsely pass source coverage. The
  checker now accepts only maximum enlargement factors of at least one.
- Vector extensions alone could bypass raster checks. Exemption now requires an
  explicit no-raster declaration and inspection pointer, still labeled attested.
- The initial short-hold fixture was visually equivalent to the clean hold.
  Separate fade intervals and frame-level regression now distinguish it.
- The original audio cue had no explicit visual event. It now follows the
  workflow transition rather than a fixed timestamp.
- An odd frame total put the cue one frame early; ceiling-based timing and a
  65-frame regression now match the actual transition.
- Visual inspection found the clean evidence title overlapped the studio label.
  The title moved into the left column; the repaired native-UHD range was inspected.
- The renderer now checks source hashes before/after rendering and runs an explicit
  browser-sRGB-to-Rec.709 conversion, rather than treating tags as conversion proof.

## Evidence Boundaries

Source-coverage arithmetic does not prove perceived sharpness. Tool probes do not
prove creative quality. Decision scenarios do not prove generated-video parity.
Contact sheets and sampled frames do not prove normal-speed motion or audio.
Missing telemetry remains unavailable. Historical evidence is archived rather
than relabeled as a fresh result.
