# Navigation Study Selection: Scorer Only

- **two-explanations:** Gives a concrete task in ordinary visitor language that does not reveal the label, control or route. Keeps the expected destination separate from the prompt. Uses plausible alternate branches in a text tree to investigate wording/grouping and a rendered first-choice test with the same task and labels, including narrow navigation. Gives different next decisions for tree failure and rendered-only failure without assigning a proven cause. Distinguishes both from completing the real task.
- **same-people:** Reports only the six observed first choices in order. Recognizes that repeat exposure or task order could have taught the destination, and that the two layouts differ by more than color. Proposes a proportionate comparison that would help distinguish the explanations (such as fresh comparable participants, or counterbalanced equivalent tasks, with a controlled variant to investigate color). Does not imply a measured conversion effect or permission to change navigation.
- **one-correct-click:** Limits the claim to one engineer's desktop first selection. Does not claim customer success, mobile performance or form completion; proposes following and verifying the intended form outcome under safe conditions, keeping audience evidence separate.
- **bounded-label:** Makes only the named copy change; preserves the destination and appearance. No research program, recruitment, tracking, taxonomy migration or redesign.
