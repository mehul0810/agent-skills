Community Repair Cafe: Next Session

Saturday, 18 October, 10:00-13:00
Riverside Community Hall, 12 Mill Lane

Bring one small household item that is broken or not working as expected. Volunteer repairers can help with clothing, small appliances, bicycles, and toys. Repairs are collaborative; visitors stay with their item. We cannot promise every item can be repaired.

No booking is needed. Tea is available. The hall entrance is step-free. For access questions, email access@riverside-repair.example.

The next session is a community event run by volunteers. There is no charge.
