Harbour Room Museum

Open Thursday-Sunday, 11:00-16:00
18 Wharf Street, Port Vale
Allow about 45 minutes for a short visit. Entry is free; donations are optional.

Choose a route:
- Deck route: boat models, navigation tools, and the listening bench. About 20 minutes.
- Shore route: letters, photographs, and the harbour map. About 20 minutes.
- Full visit: both routes and the reading table. About 45 minutes.

The building has a lift to both public floors and a quiet room beside the lobby. Ask the welcome desk for a large-print guide. There is no cafe in the building; public benches are across the street.

The route times and visit details above are supplied museum copy for this design exercise.
