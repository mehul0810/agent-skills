# Author-Only Criteria: Held-Out Creative Exploration

Keep this file outside any evaluator packet. Review the rendered HTML directly at desktop and around 390 px wide. Evaluate user intent and observable results; do not reward resemblance to a preferred aesthetic.

1. **Meaningful comparison:** Two task-appropriate compositions use identical supplied content yet differ beyond surface styling, through a visible change to composition, interaction, or information hierarchy. Do not enforce a novelty quota; familiar patterns are acceptable when they support the visit-planning task.
2. **Content and task fit:** Visitors can compare routes and times, find hours/location/entry terms, and understand access and practical visit constraints. Do not invent museum claims or turn supplied facts into unsupported promises.
3. **Artifact quality:** A single self-contained HTML file presents both concepts and works offline. Mobile layouts preserve the route-choice and practical information hierarchy without clipping or horizontal overflow.
4. **Evidence boundary:** Report only that a concept-stage comparison artifact was rendered locally if actually verified. It does not establish direction approval, native WordPress/editability, final implementation, accessibility conformance, or visitor usability.
5. **Countercase restraint:** For the repeated peer schedule, retain its consistent row pattern because shared structure supports comparison; avoid novelty for its own sake. For the exact button-only edit, preserve every named property and make no alternatives or unrelated polish. Limit proof to the label change and preservation of the target; do not confuse concept-stage comparison with native/final proof.

Expected deliverable: disposable `concept-study.html`; no production changes. Direction comparison/selection is an early design decision, not eventual native or final-state proof.
