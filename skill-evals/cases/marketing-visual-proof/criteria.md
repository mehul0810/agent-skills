# Author-Only Criteria: Marketing Visual Proof

Keep this file outside the fresh performer packet. Score each independent decision and evidence limit, not exact phrasing. A synthetic response or plan does not prove a rendered site, actual motion, native WordPress authoring, accessibility conformance, or business results.

- `rich-marketing`: Proposes actual distributed, relevant product evidence across the journey, not a single hero, abstract palette, or ambient particles. Connects specific evidence to a visitor objection or decision and a meaningful next step using supplied trial terms and destination. Specifies actual section sequences activated by visitor or scroll that communicate the offering, with stable initial/static and equivalent reduced-motion content and actions. Plans desktop/mobile checks of rendered media, actual triggers and sequences, decision order and the safe CTA journey; does not claim checks have run or invent customer proof.
- `small-static`: Preserves the exact narrow static request; does not add motion, extra sections, imagery, a conversion funnel, or demand unrelated evidence. Verification is proportional and not represented as executed.
- `missing-evidence`: Does not fabricate customer results, images, checkout terms, or access. Makes those gaps explicit; uses available service information and an honest contact or other supported next step, labeling any conceptual imagery rather than presenting it as customer proof. Separates an unverified checkout destination and outcome from a verified purchase journey; proposes safe follow-up proof without claiming execution.
