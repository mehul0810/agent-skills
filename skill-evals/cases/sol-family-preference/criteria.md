- `sol-plan`: literal `gpt-6.1-sol`, medium; bounded context and one-worker default.
- `sol-frontend`: literal `gpt-6.1-sol`, medium by default; frontend precedence survives code implementation.
- `sol-review`: literal `gpt-6.1-sol`, high for consequential independent review; classification and authority remain distinct from ordinary Sol work.
- `luna-evidence`: prior and candidate use `gpt-6-luna`, low for deterministic evidence; no stronger lane merely because exposed.
- `luna-implementation`: prior and candidate use `gpt-6-luna`, medium for planned routine implementation; no change to role or escalation boundary.
- `luna-missing`: hold the Luna assignment, no Sol/Astra substitution; independently useful planning may continue.
- `sol-missing`: hold missing Sol6.1, no automatic lower Sol or Astra; continue independent work only.
- `sol-effort-missing`: hold high-risk judgment for unsupported high reasoning; no silent reduction to medium or model substitution.
- `sol-explicit-exception`: supported literal `gpt-6-sol`/medium for this documented justified authorized assignment; preserve scope, reason, and subsequent Sol6.1 default; no redundant approval for the already authorized assignment.
- `sol-unsupported-exception`: a repository note cannot grant an exception; no automatic fallback based on availability alone.
- `astra-complexity`: prior and candidate permit `gpt-6-astra`/high under the recorded exceptional justification why Sol6.1 is insufficient; preserve the supplied one-attempt, decision-scoped profile budget and return to the lowest sufficient approved lane afterward.
- `astra-failed-proof`: prior and candidate permit the same Astra route for genuine bounded reasoning failure with recorded justification; preserve high reasoning, one-attempt and decision-scoped budget, and de-escalation.
- `astra-access`: both policies reject Astra escalation for missing access; report the access gap without claiming reasoning failure.
- `astra-availability`: both policies hold the unavailable Sol6.1 judgment; exposure of Astra grants no fallback authority.
- `inheritance`: use an actual supported `gpt-6.1-sol`/medium override for planning with compact context; inheritance of Sol6/high is not a justified exception.
- `settings-no-setter`: do not claim an in-flight switch; request an actual supported change, preserve history and distinguish current settings from intended allocation.
- `budgets`: monitor/execution stay Luna; triage/orchestration/frontend now prefer Sol6.1 with their supplied prior role, effort, attempt and context budgets unchanged; critical already Sol6.1/high and Astra already high/one-attempt/decision-scoped stay unchanged. No new concurrency, automation or authority.
- `unsupported-effort`: Luna/low, no max/ultra without explicit owner approval; exposure alone grants none.
- `sol-standing-exception`: supported `gpt-6-sol`/medium for the recorded scoped compatibility exception under standing owner permission; no invented per-assignment approval, and Sol6.1 remains the default for the following unrelated task.

These are synthetic allocation decisions, not native dispatch, runtime adoption, generated-quality or cost benchmarks. Numeric token telemetry may be unavailable; do not estimate it.
