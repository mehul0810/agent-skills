# Subject-Led Video Direction Review

## Scope And Cause

Owner feedback identified unnecessary rings and a formulaic, repeated visual
language across recent 3D videos. Inspection of their encoded frame samples and
SceneKit source found deliberately authored torus/orbit geometry, not evidence of
an encoder inventing rings. This does not rule out motion defects between the
sampled frames. No complete playback acceptance or new render is claimed.

The reusable failure is importing scene vocabulary and editorial structure from
an earlier job rather than choosing them from the current subject. The correction
separates reusable render infrastructure from creative direction and distinguishes
physical subjects, temporary explanatory guides and intentional abstraction.

## Limitations And Adoption

These evaluations measure decisions and written treatments only. They do not
establish improved finished-video creativity, clean temporal geometry, viewer
preference, universal novelty, equal model performance, savings or playback/audio
acceptance. The next commissioned video still needs its representative sequence
and complete encoded-motion review.

The installed skill points to a separate dirty primary checkout. Publication and
installed adoption are different states; activation requires a scoped sync that
preserves unrelated work. No installed-adoption claim follows from these tests.
