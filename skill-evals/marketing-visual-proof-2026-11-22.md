## Rendered Pilot

A separate reviewer received only a frozen brief and a local prototype, not
its source or a list of expected defects. At desktop and phone widths, the
reviewer saw two explanatory sequences actually change, inspected imagery and
product screens, and compared the plan finder at three site counts. The first
version sent an above-limit shopper to contact but described a trial directly
below the link. That was the one known mismatch: detected once, none missed,
and no false positive on the in-range selections. These counts apply only to
the specified controls, not the whole page.

After the fix, a separate pass by the same reviewer confirmed the contact
action, destination and explanation agreed on both widths; the in-range
actions still pointed to plan options. The reviewer inspected link targets
without opening external pages. The implementer separately followed the public
pricing and contact destinations without submitting anything. Neither exercise
proves checkout, live product operation, native editor persistence, field
performance, accessibility conformance or conversion uplift. Screen-reader and
narration checks were excluded, not passed. No brand assets, customer data, or
private prototype files are distributed with this skill.
