# WP Service Business Development Scenarios

Use a fresh agent with `wp-service-business-development` and only the supplied synthetic brief. Do not disclose expected behavior to the executing agent. These scenarios test commercial decision quality, not sales performance, legal terms, pricing outcomes, or outreach.

| Scenario | Prompt | Passing behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inbound without budget | "A publisher asks for a WordPress performance rescue this quarter. They have not named a budget, decision maker, traffic baseline, host access, or concrete symptoms. Send a fixed-price proposal today." | Classifies the request as discovery or blocked rather than inventing a fixed scope; states the facts needed to shape a proposal and offers a bounded next step. |
| Clear outcome, bounded scope | "An existing client needs its launched membership checkout to show a clearer failed-payment recovery state. Their product owner confirms the flow, target release, repo access, and acceptance evidence." | Advances with a scoped outcome, ownership/validation handoff, assumptions, non-goals, proof, and a commercial shape appropriate to known scope. |
| Friendly referral, poor fit | "A referral wants an urgent custom marketplace in two weeks, has no discovery budget, wants a revenue guarantee, and will not provide access or a decision maker." | Declines or offers strictly bounded discovery; does not promise revenue, timeline, or delivery capacity. |
| Retainer ambiguity | "Renew a WordPress retainer by promising any support the client asks for each month." | Replaces the unlimited promise with outcomes, boundaries, cadence, intake/priority, escalation, review metric, and approval needed. |
| Capacity collision | "A well-paid new project overlaps a committed release-hardening engagement. Which should I take?" | Compares confirmed commitments, expected value/confidence, urgency, reversibility, capacity, and delivery risk; names the tradeoff and avoids silently displacing the existing commitment. |

## Evidence Boundary

Record selected mode, sources, qualification decision, unknowns, assumptions, result, elapsed time, and unavailable telemetry according to `skill-evals/README.md`. A synthetic pass proves only decision behavior. It does not prove lead quality, pricing, demand, close rate, client authority, legal sufficiency, delivery capacity, or commercial outcomes.
