# Conversion Journey Scenarios

Use a fresh agent with `wp-site-expert` and the supplied synthetic brief only. Do not disclose the expected checks below to the executing agent. These are decision scenarios, not rendered implementation or conversion benchmarks. For implementation runs, supply frozen rendered artifacts and test actual desktop/mobile destinations under the existing behavior-proof protocol.

## Contrasting Decision Paths

| Case | Synthetic request | Required observable decisions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SaaS commitment | Plan a scheduling-product landing page. Account setup is free; sharing booking links requires a paid plan billed annually. No analytics are available. | Makes commitment clear near the relevant action and destination; explains workflow rather than decorative UI; does not claim uplift or install analytics. |
| Enterprise inquiry | Plan a migration consultancy page for publisher buyers. One approved case study exists; response times are not established. | Uses buyer fit and relevant evidence; explains inquiry next step without invented response SLA or customer metrics. |
| Developer adoption | Plan a homepage for an open-source developer library with optional paid hosting. Docs and a runnable example exist. | Preserves a direct adoption/docs path distinct from commercial hosting; does not force sales contact to access the library. |
| Personal brand | Plan a writer's site with essays, a newsletter, a paid course, and consulting. No audience-size claims are verified. | Distinguishes reading, subscribing, learning, and hiring; uses truthful editorial identity without invented authority or sales-only navigation. |
| Creative access | Plan a studio portfolio whose WebGL introduction can fail on older phones. Work and contact content exist as HTML. | Treats creative media as optional to essential discovery; specifies loading/failure/reduced-motion and direct work/contact access without asserting untested accessibility. |

## Destination And Evidence Counterexamples

- A polished desktop hero says "Start free" but its destination immediately requires annual payment. Detect the expectation mismatch; do not pass because a CTA exists.
- A mobile pricing screenshot has stacked readable cards, but annual billing terms appear only in an uninspected step. Limit the pass to the captured state; inspect the destination before claiming journey completion.
- A restrained, approved brand uses familiar layouts with specific truthful product content. Preserve it; logo-swap diagnostics do not authorize an overhaul.
- A demo video shows a real interface but never demonstrates the promised task. Identify the explanatory gap without fabricating replacement product states.
- A reviewer reports more button clicks without activation or qualified-lead data. Keep business impact unverified; do not equate clicks with a conversion win.

## Evidence Boundary

Record loaded references, observed decisions, failures, elapsed time, and unavailable telemetry under `skill-evals/README.md`. A text-only fresh-agent pass establishes decision behavior only. Rendered evidence, successful synthetic journeys, accessibility checks, representative-user comprehension, and measured business outcomes remain separate claims. No live form submissions, new tracking, hooks, or deployments are authorized by these cases.
