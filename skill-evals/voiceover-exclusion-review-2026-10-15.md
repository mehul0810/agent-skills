# VoiceOver Verification Exclusion Review

## Scope

The owner's standing direction excludes macOS VoiceOver screen-reader and
voice-over narration verification unless explicitly requested later. Do not
substitute another screen reader to recreate the waived gate. Omitted checks are
`excluded_by_owner`, never passed, and their absence alone does not block ordinary
completion. Keep keyboard/focus, semantics, labels, contrast, captions, automated
checks, independent review, artifact identity and all other applicable safety and
publication gates.

The visual and scoped quality contracts accept a concrete owner-directive
pointer with `claims: not_verified`. Packaged scoped accessibility proof retains
candidate-bound keyboard receipts and automated scans. Exclusion cannot erase
known defects or authorize unsupported AT, narration or WCAG claims. Video
narration omission uses the existing accepted-gap contract; unrelated technical
audio, captions, playback, rights and identity checks remain required.

## Limits

These are development decision regressions and explicitly identified synthetic
CLI/render-review checks, not held-out transfer or production proof. Fresh
fork-none dispatch and declared reads do not constitute OS-enforced isolation
or authenticated model telemetry. Host token/cost metrics were unavailable.
Actor timestamps are retained as reported; run records use the parent-observed
shared freeze-to-admission interval, including coordination and retries, not a
per-actor runtime or efficiency claim.

The synthetic geometry fixture passed 15 checks; its seeded defect failed the
768px alignment check (16px drift against a 1px tolerance). Fresh inspection of
immutable synthetic PNGs is not a new browser capture or native Pen proof.
Canonical capture/visual/spatial/asset self-tests passed. Invalid supplied
receipts were rejected; one malformed historical visual fixture produced an
existing missing-`assetReceipts` TypeError, not a structured diagnostic or a
passing result. That diagnostic limitation remains outside this scoped change.

No VoiceOver, substitute-reader, narration, native Pen, conformance, production,
release, tag or deployment verification was performed. Repository/template
publication is not installation into every existing project: original dirty
checkouts and installed skill links were not overwritten. Existing consumer
harness pins remain unchanged; compatibility checks, not repinning, validate
this guidance/template-only harness change.
