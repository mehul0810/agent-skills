## Scope

The run record is a development regression, not evidence that a website was
rendered or usable. In a separate local pilot, the input was two public
homepages and the deliverable a new design for the second brand. The reviewer
followed a labeled pricing link and found that the actual comparison lived at a
different destination. Following the journey caught something a palette study
or static screenshot could not. No brand assets or private pilot files are
distributed here. Neither the decision exercise nor the pilot establishes
conversion, field performance, native editor parity, or accessibility
conformance. Screen-reader and narration checks were excluded, not passed.
